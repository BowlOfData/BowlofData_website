You receive a one-page scope: deliverables, what we need from you, how long it takes. Nothing starts until you agree to it.

Why work with us

We run what we sell

Bowl of Data is produced every week by Maki, our open-source multi-agent pipeline. It reads hundreds of sources, ranks and summarises them, and has not missed an issue. Retrieval, evaluation and review are things we do weekly, not things we write slides about.

Regulated-industry background

Marco has worked inside banks and insurers on IT, data and model risk. The assurance work is written for the people who have to defend it to an auditor, not for a slide deck.

Private by design

We run mainly on open models on our own hardware. When your data cannot go to a third-party AI service, we can build and test without sending it there.

Small team, fixed scope

You work with the people who do the work. Every engagement starts with a written scope and ends with something you can keep.
