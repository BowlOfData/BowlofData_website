For anyone putting agents or LLM applications in front of real data and real tools, whether the model is private or a commercial API.

Agent & Guardrail Security Review

What your agents can do, what your models can say, and what you can prove afterwards.

Fixed scope · typically 2–3 weeks

  • Audit logging and blocking rules for unauthorized tool calls and agent actions

  • Prioritised findings you can act on

AI Vendor Report Review

Due diligence on the AI providers you rely on, read from their SOC 2 reports.

Fixed scope · typically 1–2 weeks

  • Structured read of a vendor’s SOC 2 report: scope, period, exceptions, subservice organisations
  • The controls the report expects you to operate yourself
  • Gaps against your ICT third-party risk expectations (DORA Articles 28 to 30)
  • A findings memo for your vendor file

A review of the report. Not an audit and not an attestation.

Why work with us

We run what we sell

Bowl of Data is produced every week by Maki, our open-source multi-agent pipeline. It reads hundreds of sources, ranks and summarises them, and has not missed an issue. Retrieval, evaluation and review are things we do weekly, not things we write slides about. Our security work follows the same pattern: Maki’s built-in plugins are gated by allowlists, and because Maki is open source you can read how.

Regulated-industry background

Marco has worked inside banks and insurers on IT, data and model risk. The assurance work is written for the people who have to defend it to an auditor, not for a slide deck.

Private by design

We run mainly on open models on our own hardware. When your data cannot go to a third-party AI service, we can build and test without sending it there.

Small team, fixed scope

You work with the people who do the work. Every engagement starts with a written scope and ends with something you can keep.
