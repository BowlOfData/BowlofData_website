AI guardrails for agents, LLM apps and RAG

Guardrails are the controls around a model: what an agent is allowed to do, what gets logged, what gets blocked, and what the model may say. We review whether yours exist, whether they hold, and what evidence they leave, for private and commercial AI and for RAG architectures.

Common questions

What are AI guardrails?
Controls that limit and record what an AI system can do: permissions on the tools an agent may call, rules that block unauthorized actions, logs that show what happened, and safeguards on the content a model produces.
Can guardrails stop all harmful or inaccurate output?
No. We can reduce it and show you the evidence. No safeguard catches everything, so the review also sets how failures are detected and handled.
Do you implement the guardrails too?
The review comes first and ends in prioritised findings. Implementing the controls is scoped separately once the review is done.
Does it apply to a private model as well as a commercial API?
Yes. The review covers agents and LLM applications whether the model is private or a commercial API.
