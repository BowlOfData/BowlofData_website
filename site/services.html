Common questions

What is a governance readiness assessment?
A fixed-scope review of where your IT, data and AI governance stands. We build an inventory of your AI and model-based systems, classify each by risk, compare the result with what MaRisk and DORA expect, and give you a documentation roadmap ordered by what an auditor will ask for first. It typically takes three to four weeks.
What does an LLM and model risk review cover?
An evaluation framework for one model or LLM application, mapped to MaRisk and DORA expectations, and to BAIT where it still applies. It covers accuracy, robustness, bias and data-leakage tests, validation evidence written for model risk and audit teams, and monitoring and re-validation triggers.
What does the agent and guardrail security review cover?
It looks at what your agents and LLM applications can reach and do: which tools and actions each agent is allowed, whether unauthorized tool calls and agent actions are logged and blocked, and what guardrails sit around private and commercial AI and RAG architectures. It typically takes two to three weeks and ends in prioritised findings. Implementing the controls is scoped separately afterwards.
Can you stop harmful or inaccurate output?
We can reduce it and show you the evidence. Content safeguards for toxic, biased or inaccurate output are part of the security review, and measuring accuracy is part of the evaluation work. No safeguard catches everything, so the review also sets how failures are detected and handled.
What does “optimized RAG pipeline” mean in practice?
A retrieval-augmented generation setup whose chunking, indexing and ranking are tuned on your own questions and measured against an evaluation set, so quality is shown rather than assumed. Every answer carries its sources and an audit trail.
What is private AI, and can you implement it?
Private AI means models that run where your data stays: on your hardware or private cloud, with no third-party AI service in the loop. We run mainly on open models on our own hardware every week, and we implement the same approach for clients, scoped per project.
What does the AI vendor report review involve, and is it an audit?
We read an AI vendor's SOC 2 report in a structured way: the scope, the period covered, any exceptions, the subservice organisations, and the controls the report expects you to operate yourself. We then compare the gaps with your ICT third-party risk expectations, including DORA Articles 28 to 30, and give you a findings memo for your vendor file. It is a review of the report. It is not an audit and not an attestation.
Is our data kept private?
Yes. We run mainly on open models on our own hardware, and we can build and test on your infrastructure, so your documents are not handed to a commercial AI API to log or train on.
Who does the work?
